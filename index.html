🙏 About Adi Kailash - The Mini Kailash

Adi Kailash, also known as "Chota Kailash" or the Mini Kailash, is a sacred pilgrimage destination in the Himalayas. Located in the mystical regions of Pithoragarh in Uttarakhand, this holy site is believed to be the abode of Lord Shiva. The pristine mountain peak, surrounded by ancient temples and breathtaking landscapes, offers a spiritual sanctuary for devotees and trekkers seeking divine connection and natural beauty.

Popular Pilgrimage Routes

🏔️ Classic Adi Kailash Trek

Duration: 6-7 Days | Difficulty: Moderate

The traditional pilgrimage route starting from Dharchula. Experience the spiritual essence of Adi Kailash with visits to sacred temples, meditation caves, and the stunning Panchachuli peaks. Includes stays at pilgrim lodges and authentic mountain experiences.

⛰️ Extended Himalayan Yatra

Duration: 10-12 Days | Difficulty: Moderate-Hard

An immersive spiritual journey combining Adi Kailash with visits to nearby sacred sites including Panchchuli Glacier and ancient temples. Deep spiritual experiences with extended meditation and exploration of Himalayan heritage.

🧘 Spiritual Retreat Package

Duration: 5-7 Days | Difficulty: Easy-Moderate

Perfect for spiritual seekers. Includes guided meditations, yoga sessions, Vedic teachings, and temple rituals at sacred sites. Comfortable accommodations with flexibility for personal spiritual practices and rejuvenation.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Pilgrimage Package

Duration: 5-6 Days | Difficulty: Easy

A family-friendly pilgrimage balancing spiritual experiences with comfort. Includes scenic stops, cultural experiences, and flexible pacing suitable for children and elderly family members.
